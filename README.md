# AI & ML in the Real World 🌍🤖

## About This Project

This project identifies five real-world problems that can be solved or improved using Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML).

## Problems and Solutions

### 1. Agriculture 🌾

**Problem:** Crop diseases can cause significant losses for farmers.

**AI/ML Solution:** Use image recognition to identify possible crop diseases from photographs of plant leaves.

**Expected Benefit:** Earlier disease detection and reduced crop losses.

### 2. Healthcare 🏥

**Problem:** Some diseases are detected too late.

**AI/ML Solution:** Develop AI tools to support healthcare professionals in identifying patients who may need further screening.

**Expected Benefit:** Potentially earlier diagnosis and treatment.

### 3. Education 📚

**Problem:** Students have different learning needs and learning speeds.

**AI/ML Solution:** Create personalised learning systems that recommend lessons and practice exercises.

**Expected Benefit:** Improved learning experiences and quicker feedback.

### 4. Transportation 🚗

**Problem:** Traffic congestion wastes time and fuel.

**AI/ML Solution:** Use machine learning to predict traffic and help optimise traffic signals.

**Expected Benefit:** Better traffic flow and reduced fuel consumption.

### 5. Environment 🌍

**Problem:** Improper waste sorting reduces recycling efficiency.

**AI/ML Solution:** Use computer vision to identify and sort recyclable materials.

**Expected Benefit:** Improved recycling and reduced landfill waste.

## Best Idea 💡

My favourite idea is AI-based crop disease detection because it could help farmers identify plant diseases early and protect their crops.

## Conclusion

AI and ML can help address real-world challenges across agriculture, healthcare, education, transportation, and environmental protection. Responsible implementation, data privacy, and human oversight are essential.
